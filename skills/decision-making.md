# Decision and escalation skill

First collect the conflicting proposals and evidence. Ask Leaders to seek consensus. Escalate unresolved departmental issues to HODs, cross-department issues to Principals, and only high-impact critical unresolved matters to the single Master Leader. At each level, evaluate evidence, safety, reversibility, cost, and user requirements. If still unresolved, ask the owner; do not fabricate consensus or silently execute irreversible changes.
